Question: Urban areas without cars would be better for the air we breathe. Do you agree?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I completely agree that urban areas without cars would significantly improve the air we breathe. In recent years, air pollution has become one of the most dangerous threats to public health. Vehicles emit harmful greenhouse gasses every day, damaging our ecosystems and causing severe respiratory diseases in crowded cities.

If cities banned private cars from central areas, citizens would use public transport, bicycles, or walk. Many European cities have already tested car-free zones, and the results show cleaner air and safer streets. In conclusion, reducing car traffic in urban areas is an essential step to protect our environment and ensure a healthier future.
