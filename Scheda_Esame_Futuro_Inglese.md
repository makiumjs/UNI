## 🧭 Mappa Decisionale Rapida (Come scegliere in 3 secondi)

```
Cosa esprime l'azione futura?
│
├── 📅 Orario ufficiale, treno, aereo, calendario pubblico?
│   └── ➔ PRESENT SIMPLE (es. "The train leaves at 11:30")
│
├── 🤝 Impegno già fissato, appuntamento in agenda, prenotazione fatta?
│   └── ➔ PRESENT CONTINUOUS (es. "I am seeing my dentist tomorrow")
│
├── 🧠 Intenzione personale decisa PRIMA ma non ancora organizzata?
│   └── ➔ BE GOING TO (es. "We are going to hire a car")
│
├── 👁️ Previsione basata su un INDIZIO VISIBILE / EVIDENZA PRESENTE?
│   └── ➔ BE GOING TO (es. "Look at those clouds! It is going to rain")
│
├── ⚡ Decisione spontanea presa ORA, offerta, promessa, rifiuto?
│   └── ➔ FUTURE SIMPLE / WILL (es. "Don't panic. I'll help you")
│
├── 🔮 Previsione basata su OPINIONE PERSONALE (think, sure, hope, probably)?
│   └── ➔ FUTURE SIMPLE / WILL (es. "I think Tom will pass the exam")
│
├── ⏳ Azione in pieno svolgimento in un momento preciso del futuro?
│   └── ➔ FUTURE CONTINUOUS (es. "This time tomorrow I will be flying to London")
│
└── ⏱️ Azione subordinata dopo IF, WHEN, AS SOON AS, UNLESS, BEFORE, AFTER?
    └── ➔ PRESENT SIMPLE nella secondaria + WILL nella principale (First Conditional)
```

## 1. Present Simple (con valore di futuro)

* **Formula:** Forma base / 3ª persona singolare con `-s` o `-es`.
* **Nome della struttura per l'aperta:** `Present Simple`
* **Quando si usa (definizione esatta slide Lez. 23):**
  > *"We use the Present Simple to talk about timetables, programmes and official schedules (public transport, flights, trains, cinemas, conferences)."*
* **Segnali / Triggers:** orari precisi (`at 10:30`, `at 7:15`), giorni fissati da tabellone, verbi come *arrive, leave, start, begin, finish, depart*.
* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *"Bianca's flight **arrives** (arrive) at 10:30."*
  * *"My train **leaves** at 11.30, so I need to be at the station by 11.15."*
  * *"What time **does** the film **begin** this evening?"*
* **Trappola d'esame:** Non usare MAI `will` per orari di treni o aerei (`The train will leave at 10` è SBAGLIATO).

---

## 2. Present Continuous (con valore di futuro)

* **Formula:** `am / is / are + verbo-ing`
* **Nome della struttura per l'aperta:** `Present Continuous` (for future arrangements)
* **Quando si usa (definizione esatta slide Lez. 23):**
  > *"We use the Present Continuous to refer to events which are arranged for the future, where we have already decided AND arranged to do them (appointments, bookings, shared plans)."*
* **Segnali / Triggers:** menzione di appuntamenti dal medico, biglietti acquistati, hotel prenotati (`I have an appointment`, `I booked a room`, `tomorrow at 5`).
* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *"We **are travelling** (travel) to Greece on Wednesday morning. I have booked a room at a fancy hotel."*
  * *"I **am seeing** (see) my doctor tomorrow morning at 9. I have an appointment."*
  * *"We **are taking** our finals tomorrow morning."*
  * *"Eric and Julie **are going** to the theatre this evening."*
* **Trappola d'esame:** Ricorda il raddoppio in inglese UK: *travelling* (US: *traveling*). Se c'è *appointment* o *booked*, la risposta è SEMPRE Present Continuous, MAI *will* o *going to*.

## 3. Be Going To

* **Formula:** `am / is / are + going to + bare infinitive` (forma base)
* **Nome della struttura per l'aperta:** `be going to` (intention / prediction based on present evidence)
* **Quando si usa (2 casi distinti):**
  1. **Intenzioni e progetti:** *"To talk about plans and intentions decided before speaking, which may still change and are not yet fully arranged."*
  2. **Previsioni con prova evidente:** *"To describe a prediction based on something we can see or know about right now (present evidence)."*
* **Segnali / Triggers:**
  * Per le intenzioni: *already decided, plan to, haven't arranged it yet*.
  * Per le previsioni: *Look at those clouds!*, *Look at that vase!*, *looks pale*, *does not know anything*.
* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *"Luisa **is going to** finish her paper this evening."* (Intenzione)
  * *"We **are going to** spend our holiday in Austria."* (Piano non ancora formalizzato)
  * *"Look at those clouds. It **is going to** rain."* (Evidenza visiva)
  * *"That vase **is going to** fall on your head."* (Evidenza immediata)
  * *"Paula does not know anything. She **is going to** fail the exam."* (Fatto noto nel presente)
* **Trappola d'esame:** Non dimenticare il `to`! Dire *"Luisa is going finish"* è errato.

## 4. Future Simple (Will / Won't)

* **Formula:** `will / won't + bare infinitive` (infinito senza *to*)
* **Nome della struttura per l'aperta:** `Future Simple` (will-future)
* **Quando si usa (2 casi fondamentali):**
  1. **Decisioni spontanee e reazioni al momento del parlato:** *"To express a future intention or decision made at the moment of speaking (offers, promises, requests, refusals)."*
  2. **Previsioni basate su opinioni / convinzioni personali:** *"To describe a prediction or something we think/believe will happen in the future (without present physical evidence)."*
* **Segnali / Triggers:**
  * Spontaneo: *Don't panic!*, *The phone is ringing*, *Don't worry*, *I promise*.
  * Opinione: *I think*, *I don't think*, *I'm sure*, *probably*, *perhaps*, *I hope*.
* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *“I'm tired, and I still have a lot to do.” “Don't panic. **I'll help** you.”* (Offerta spontanea)
  * *“The phone is ringing.” “**I'll pick** it up for you.”* (Decisione al momento)
  * *“I can't buy this suit.” “Don't worry, **I'll lend** you some money.”* (Promessa/offerta)
  * *"Tom **will pass** the exam easily."* / *"I'm sure your teacher **will be** angry."* (Previsione/opinione)
* **Regola d'oro delle slide:** *"Do NOT use will to talk about what you have already decided or arranged to do!"*

---

## 5. Future Continuous

* **Formula:** `will be + verbo-ing`
* **Nome della struttura per l'aperta:** `Future Continuous`
* **Quando si usa (definizione slide Lez. 23):**
  1. **Azione in corso in un momento futuro specifico:** *"To talk about activities which will be in progress at a certain time in the future."*
  2. **Routine o eventi regolari già pianificati:** *"To talk about planned routines and regular events."*
* **Segnali / Triggers:** *This time tomorrow*, *This time next week*, *at 10 tomorrow*, *when you arrive*.
* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *"This time tomorrow they **will be celebrating** Peter’s birthday."*
  * *"Tomorrow I **will be sleeping** all day."*
  * *"He **will be waiting** for you when you arrive."*
  * *"This time next week we **will be staying** in this beautiful hotel."*

## 6. Il Futuro nelle Proposizioni Temporali & First Conditional (Lez. 24)

Questa è la trappola grammaticale numero uno nei test e nelle domande aperte:

### La Regola di Ferro:
> Nelle proposizioni secondarie introdotte da congiunzioni temporali o ipotetiche:
> **`IF / WHEN / AS SOON AS / UNLESS / AS LONG AS / BEFORE / AFTER / IN CASE`**  
> **NON si usa MAI `will`! Si usa il PRESENT SIMPLE (o Present Perfect).**  
> Il `will` va esclusivamente nella proposizione PRINCIPALE.

* **Schema First Conditional:**
  $$\text{if / as soon as / when} + \text{Present Simple} \longrightarrow \text{will} + \text{bare infinitive}$$

* **Esempi ufficiali d'esame:**
  * *"I **'ll call** (call) you as soon as I **get** home."* (NON *"as soon as I will get home"*)
  * *"I **'ll call** you as soon as your package **arrives**."*
  * *"I’ll take my umbrella in case it **rains**."*
  * *"If it **is** sunny tomorrow, we **'ll go** to the park."*
  * *"When I **finish** work, I **'ll call** you."*
  * *"I'll go home after I **have done** the shopping."*

## 7. Tabella Sinottica Comparativa

| Struttura Grammaticale | Formula | Caso d'Uso Principale | Frase Esempio Chiave |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present Simple** | `V / V-(e)s` | Orari ufficiali, trasporti, programmi pubblici | *The flight **arrives** at 10:30.* |
| **Present Continuous** | `am/is/are + V-ing` | Piani concordati, impegni fissati, appuntamenti | *I **am seeing** my doctor at 9.* |
| **Be Going To (Piano)** | `am/is/are going to + V` | Intenzione decisa ma non ancora organizzata | *We **are going to** hire a car.* |
| **Be Going To (Segno)** | `am/is/are going to + V` | Previsione con evidenza presente o visibile | *Look at the clouds, it **is going to** rain.* |
| **Future Simple (Spontaneo)** | `will + V` | Scelta al momento, offerta, promessa, aiuto | *Don't panic, I **will help** you.* |
| **Future Simple (Opinione)** | `will + V` | Previsione soggettiva (*think, hope, sure*) | *I think you **will pass** the test.* |
| **Future Continuous** | `will be + V-ing` | Azione in svolgimento a un'ora precisa futura | *This time tomorrow I **will be flying**.* |
| **Subordinata Temporale** | `as soon as / when + Pres. Simple` | Condizione o evento scatenante nel futuro | *I'll call you as soon as I **arrive**.* |

## 📝 Come Rispondere all'Esame alle Domande Aperte sui Verbi

Se ti capita una domanda come quella della Lezione 23:
> *"Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb. What is the name of the structure and when is it used?*  
> *We ________ (travel) to Greece on Wednesday morning. I have booked a room at a fancy hotel."*

Struttura la tua risposta in 3 righe precise per ottenere il massimo punteggio (3/3 punti):
1. **Frase completata:**  
   `We are travelling to Greece on Wednesday morning. I have booked a room at a fancy hotel.`
2. **Nome della struttura:**  
   `Name of the structure: Present Continuous (used with future meaning).`
3. **Regola d'uso:**  
   `When it is used: It is used to refer to fixed events or arrangements in the future that have already been decided and organized in detail (here confirmed by having already booked the hotel).`
